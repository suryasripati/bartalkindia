What we could not find

Whisky vs rum by state? Spirits vs beer by state? Nobody publishes it for free. The trackers sit behind paywalls, so we left it blank instead of guessing. If you have a source, the next round is on you.

Tax collected is not litres drunk. Per-head figures divide by all residents, children included, and visitors in Goa, Delhi or Puducherry inflate theirs. "Best estimate" adds VAT and other liquor layers where sources allow (NIPFP shares, state budgets, an industry-body cross-check) and carries real uncertainty, widest for Kerala, West Bengal and a few smaller states. Drinking data is self-reported and has sampling error; Manipur is missing from NFHS-6. Sources: RBI State Finances (2021-22 to 2025-26), NIPFP WP 415, ISWAI via CAG, NFHS-6 fact sheets, Technical Group population projections 2025.

Sources and cautions

State excise: RBI, State Finances: A Study of Budgets (2021-22 to 2025-26 editions), Appendix I. FY21 is a revised estimate because the 2020-21 Accounts edition was unavailable. VAT shares: NIPFP Working Paper 415 (2024). Cross-check: ISWAI (an industry body) alcohol share of state own tax revenue, via CAG; unverified and not independent. Drinking prevalence: NFHS-6 state fact sheets (MoHFW/IIPS, 2023-24); survey sampling error applies, and Manipur is absent. Population: Technical Group on Population Projections, 2025.

Best-estimate confidence runs from A (confirmed structure) to C (unknown VAT layer); Kerala has three conflicting figures from press reports (low ₹14,822 cr, high ₹18,774 cr); West Bengal and Arunachal have source conflicts. Tax per head is state-wide and includes visitors' purchases (Goa, Puducherry, Delhi and Haryana look higher for that reason). Alcohol is outside GST, so there is no single national series.

Sources: RBI State Finances (2021-22 to 2025-26), NIPFP WP 415, ISWAI via CAG, NFHS-6 state fact sheets, Technical Group population projections 2025. Tax collected is not litres drunk; see Analyst mode for ranges and caveats.
